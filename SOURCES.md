# Authoritative sources and applicability

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

The skill methods use these public primary sources. Always confirm the current
version and the requirements applicable to the organization, jurisdiction,
industry, data, service, and contract.

## Baseline sources

### NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1 Update 1

- Source: [Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management Practices for Systems and
  Organizations](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/161/r1/upd1/final)
- Publisher: National Institute of Standards and Technology
- Publication: May 2022; updates through November 2024
- Use in this repository: lifecycle risk identification, assessment,
  treatment, multilevel governance, supplier requirements, and integration of
  cybersecurity supply-chain risk into enterprise risk management.
- Applicability: voluntary guidance unless adopted through policy, contract,
  regulation, or another authority.

### NIST SP 1305

- Source: [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0: Quick-Start Guide for Cybersecurity
  Supply Chain Risk Management](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1305/final)
- Publisher: National Institute of Standards and Technology
- Publication: October 2024
- Use in this repository: the CSF 2.0 Govern supply-chain outcomes, supplier
  requirements, roles, prioritization, and ongoing capability operation.
- Applicability: voluntary guidance; tailor to mission, risk appetite, and
  operating context.

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 Core

- Source: [The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
  2.0](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.CSWP.29)
- Publisher: National Institute of Standards and Technology
- Publication: February 2024
- Use in this repository: outcome identifiers and language, especially
  Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management (`GV.SC`), Risk Management
  Strategy (`GV.RM`), Oversight (`GV.OV`), Risk Assessment (`ID.RA`),
  Improvement (`ID.IM`), incident response, and recovery.
- Mapping rule: a skill output may support a CSF outcome; completing the skill
  does not by itself establish implementation, effectiveness, or conformity.

### CISA ICT supply-chain resources

- Source: [Reducing ICT Supply Chain Risk with Security and Resilience
  Considerations](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/reducing-ict-supply-chain-risk-small-and-medium-sized-businesses-fact-sheet)
- Source: [Operationalizing Vendor SCRM Template for
  SMBs](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/operationalizing-vendor-scrm-template-smbs)
- Publisher: Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency
- Use in this repository: supplier visibility, inventory, disruption planning,
  practical vendor risk questions, and operationalization.
- Applicability: practical U.S. government guidance; not a certification or
  complete control framework.

## Regulated overlay

### Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management

- Source: [Federal Reserve SR 23-4 / CA 23-5](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2304.htm)
- Detailed source: [Interagency
  Guidance](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/interagency-guidance-on-third-party-relationships.htm)
- Publishers: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, Federal Deposit
  Insurance Corporation, and Office of the Comptroller of the Currency
- Publication: June 2023
- Use in this repository: planning, due diligence and selection, contract
  negotiation, ongoing monitoring, termination, inventory, critical activities,
  governance, documentation, and independent review.
- Applicability: issued for supervised banking organizations. Treat it as an
  optional regulated overlay outside that population. Do not represent it as a
  universal legal requirement.

## Interpretation rules

- Label statements from a source as guidance unless an applicable authority
  makes them mandatory.
- Do not claim framework compliance from use of these skills.
- Record organization-specific policy, regulatory, contractual, and risk
  appetite requirements separately from baseline guidance.
- Resolve conflicts in favor of applicable law, regulation, contract, policy,
  and authorized governance.
- Revalidate source currency before relying on the repository for a material
  production decision.
