# Contract control rules

Draft operational requirements, not final legal language.

## Measurability

Prefer: actor + required outcome + scope + timing + evidence + exception path.

Weak: "Supplier will maintain industry-standard security."

Better operational requirement: "Supplier must maintain MFA for privileged
administrative access to the in-scope service and provide annual evidence or
equivalent independent assurance, subject to counsel-approved wording."

## Triggered domains

- Data: authorized use, locations, protection, retention, return, deletion.
- Access: least privilege, authentication, personnel, logging, customer duties.
- Assurance: scoped evidence, audit/assessment rights, remediation.
- Incident: definition, notice clock, content, updates, preservation,
  cooperation, regulatory/customer support.
- Resilience: RTO/RPO, testing, dependencies, notification of failure.
- Vulnerability: testing, disclosure, severity, remediation, emergency action.
- Subprocessors: notice, objection/approval as applicable, flow-down, liability.
- Exit: transition, formats, access removal, deletion attestation, survival.

Use calendar days or hours, starting event, recipient, and required evidence
where timing matters. Route liability, indemnity, insurance, governing law,
regulatory wording, and enforceability to counsel.
