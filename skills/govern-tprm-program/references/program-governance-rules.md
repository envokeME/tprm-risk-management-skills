# Program governance rules

## Minimum operating model

- Strategy and policy aligned to enterprise risk.
- Complete inventory with risk prioritization and named business ownership.
- Proportionate planning, diligence, contracting, monitoring, renewal, and exit.
- Cross-functional roles and delegated decision authorities.
- Findings, exceptions, acceptance, expiry, and escalation workflow.
- Critical-service, fourth-party, and concentration oversight.
- Incident, response, recovery, continuity, and exit integration.
- Management reporting, independent review, training, and improvement.

## Metric design

Balance:

- Coverage: population discovered, owned, tiered, and in a lifecycle state.
- Timeliness: decisions before use, overdue evidence/findings, renewal lead time.
- Quality: rework, unsupported conclusions, QA failures, stale decisions.
- Risk: outside-appetite exposure, exceptions, incident trends, concentration.
- Outcomes: remediation verified, recovery demonstrated, exits tested.

Define numerator, denominator, source, owner, cadence, threshold, limitation,
and action. Do not use activity counts alone as evidence of effectiveness.

## Lines of accountability

Business owners own the relationship and operational dependency. Subject-matter
functions evaluate their domains. TPRM coordinates and challenges. Authorized
risk owners accept residual risk. Internal audit or another suitably independent
function evaluates program design and effectiveness according to mandate.
