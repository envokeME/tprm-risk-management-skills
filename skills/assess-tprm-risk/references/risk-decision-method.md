# Risk decision method

## Readiness

Classify unresolved items:

- `Blocking`: could change a critical decision or violates a mandatory gate.
- `Decision under uncertainty`: cannot reasonably be resolved now; quantify or
  describe the range and escalate.
- `Non-blocking`: low influence; monitor or close with rationale.

## Risk statement

Use this form:

> Because [condition or event], [asset/objective] may experience [impact],
> supported by [evidence], with [uncertainty].

Avoid vague labels such as "vendor cyber risk."

## Residual assessment

1. Start from inherent exposure.
2. Credit only relevant controls with sufficient evidence.
3. Consider control dependencies and complementary user responsibilities.
4. Apply likelihood and impact criteria from organization policy.
5. Record confidence and a plausible adverse rating when uncertainty is high.
6. Apply mandatory overrides before aggregation.

Do not create an arithmetic formula that hides a critical condition.

## Treatment quality

Every action needs an accountable owner, due date, measurable completion
criterion, validation evidence, and consequence if missed. Separate:

- prerequisites before use;
- time-bound post-approval actions;
- ongoing conditions;
- accepted residual exposure with authority and expiry.

Acceptance should be explicit, within delegated authority, time-bound where
appropriate, and linked to monitoring or reconsideration triggers.
