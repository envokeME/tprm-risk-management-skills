# Assess TPRM Risk

## Objective

Create a decision record that shows what can happen, why, the impact, current
support, remaining uncertainty, proposed treatment, and required authority.

## Required inputs

- Scoped engagement and inherent-risk tier
- Evidence review record with citations and unresolved follow-ups
- Applicable risk criteria, appetite, policy, and approval authority
- Business benefit, alternatives, timeline, and existing commitments

## Workflow

1. Check decision readiness using `references/risk-decision-method.md`. If a
   blocking fact can reasonably be obtained, return for targeted follow-up.
2. Write each risk as condition or event, affected asset/objective, and impact.
3. Link causes and current controls to evidence statuses. Do not treat planned
   remediation as implemented control.
4. Assess likelihood and impact with organization criteria; state uncertainty
   and avoid unsupported numerical precision.
5. Determine residual risk after supported controls only. Apply policy
   overrides and aggregation or concentration considerations.
6. Compare options: avoid, reduce, transfer/share, accept, or defer. Include
   owner, due date, validation evidence, and interim protection.
7. Recommend `Approve`, `Approve with conditions`, `Defer`, or `Decline` only
   when the organization's process uses those outcomes.
8. Copy `assets/residual-risk-decision.md`. Leave approval fields unsigned for
   the accountable authority.

## NIST alignment

Use `references/nist-alignment.md` for NIST CSF 2.0 `GV.SC-03`, `GV.SC-07`,
`GV.RM-06`, and `ID.RA-05`, supported by the multilevel risk approach in NIST
SP 800-161 Rev. 1.

## Output contract

Produce prioritized, evidence-linked risk statements; residual ratings with
confidence; open uncertainty; treatment plan; recommendation; policy exceptions;
and named approval route.

## Boundaries

- Do not fabricate evidence, ratings criteria, legal conclusions, or approval.
- Do not present the recommendation as an approval or sign for a risk owner.
- Do not convert missing evidence to a control failure without support; model
  it as uncertainty and explain the decision consequence.
- Escalate risk outside appetite, unresolved critical exposure, or authority
  conflict.
- Keep risk acceptance and final approval with an authorized human.

## Quality check

Confirm the decision can be challenged from the record: evidence, uncertainty,
rating basis, alternatives, conditions, authority, and expiry are visible.
