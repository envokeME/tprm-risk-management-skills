# Renewal and exit rules

## Renewal minimums

Before recommending renewal:

- confirm scope, data, access, use, locations, owners, and criticality;
- review incidents, outages, SLA and recovery performance;
- test condition and finding closure with evidence;
- refresh risk-significant assurance;
- review contract gaps, subprocessors, concentration, and financial condition;
- confirm risk authority and any acceptance expiry;
- assess credible alternatives and exit readiness.

Do not treat uninterrupted service as proof of adequate control.

## Exit workstreams

1. Governance: decision, notice, owners, counsel, communications.
2. Continuity: replacement, migration, rollback, recovery, customer impact.
3. Identity and technology: accounts, federation, tokens, keys, certificates,
   network paths, agents, repositories, devices, licenses.
4. Data and records: authoritative inventory, return format, validated receipt,
   retention basis, deletion method, backups, subprocessor copies, attestation.
5. Commercial/legal: fees, assets, IP, disputes, surviving terms, legal holds.
6. Validation: access tests, reconciliation, deletion evidence, residual risks,
   post-exit monitoring, lessons learned.

For emergency exits, prioritize harm containment and continuity, record skipped
steps, and assign time-bound recovery actions.
